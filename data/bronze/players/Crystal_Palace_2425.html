The 2024–25 season was the 119th season in the history of Crystal Palace Football Club, and their twelfth consecutive season in the Premier League. In addition to the domestic league, the club also participated in the FA Cup and the EFL Cup.

Marc Guéhi was appointed club captain ahead of the season, replacing Joel Ward.[2] This was the first season since 2015–16 not to feature James Tomkins, who departed the club in the summer of 2024 before retiring. Michael Olise also departed for Bayern Munich after three years for a club record sale of £50.8 million.

On 26 April 2025, Crystal Palace beat Aston Villa 3–0 in the FA Cup semi-finals to make the final for the third time in their history. They would go on to beat Manchester City 1–0 in the final on 17 May to win the FA Cup for the first time, which marked the Eagles' first ever major trophy and qualified them for the 2025 FA Community Shield as well as the play-off round of the 2025–26 UEFA Conference League, after they were demoted from 2025–26 UEFA Europa League league phase due to multi-club ownership with fellow participant Olympique Lyonnais. They also finished the season on 53 points, their highest ever in the Premier League.

FA Cup

[edit]
Main article: 2024–25 FA Cup

Crystal Palace won the FA Cup, defeating fellow Premier League team Manchester City in the final, 1–0, with a goal from Eberechi Eze.

As a Premier League club, Palace entered the FA Cup in the third round, and were drawn at home to League One side Stockport County.[79][80] They were then drawn away against League Two club Doncaster Rovers in the fourth round,[81][82] at home to Championship side Millwall in the fifth round[83][84] and away to Premier League side Fulham in the quarter-finals.[85] In the semi-finals, they were drawn against Premier League club Aston Villa.[86]

EFL Cup

[edit]
Main article: 2024–25 EFL Cup

As a Premier League club not competing in European competitions, Palace entered the EFL Cup in the second round, and were drawn at home to EFL Championship side Norwich City.[87][88] They were then drawn away to Queens Park Rangers in the third round,[89][90] away to Aston Villa in the fourth round,[91] and away to Arsenal in the quarter-finals.[92]
