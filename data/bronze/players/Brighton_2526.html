The 2025–26 season was the 124th season in the history of Brighton & Hove Albion, and their ninth consecutive season in the Premier League. In addition to the domestic league, the club also participated in the FA Cup and the EFL Cup.[1]

Brighton finished the league season in eighth place, qualifying for European football in the Conference League play-off stage.[2]
